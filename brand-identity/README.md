# StreetReader: A Solarpunk Intervention

**Thesis Goal:** Re-reading Gran Vía's sustainability through a sensory lens.

### The Four Pillars[cite: 1]:
1. **Ecological Integration:** Reconnecting the street to nature beyond metrics.
2. **Spatial Justice:** questioning who the street is actually for.
3. **Organic Design:** Moving away from rigid, "high-performance" corridor layouts.
4. **Participatory Agency:** Allowing lived data to drive institutional change.
